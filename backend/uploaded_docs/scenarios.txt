# Scenario Name: DG_APH_01
# Title: Account high velocity ATM withdrawals.

## Scenario Metadata
Category :		ATM/Phone activity (DG_APH)
Frequency :	Daily

Subject: Account 

## Descriptions
Short description:	Account high velocity ATM withdrawals.
Long description:	An account has a total amount of ATM withdrawals greater than a large percentage of deposits made in the same time period.
Detailed description:	This scenario calculates the sum of ATM withdrawals and deposits during the specified time period. The scenario also calculates that account's sum of ATM withdrawals occurring since the earliest deposit date. If that sum meets or exceeds the specified percentage of deposits, a match is found.
Dependent view(s):	DGINVEST_PARTY_ACCOUNT_TRANSACTIONS
View level(s):	1 level
Scenario message:	Total Deposits Amount= #1; Total ATM Withdrawals Amount=  #2
Features used:	Normal
Technical notes:	None
